# Code of Conduct

## In short

Be decent. We are here to build useful tools, not to score points.

## Expected

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and non-native speakers.
- Critique code, not people. "This leaks user input" is feedback; "this is garbage" is not.
- Assume good intent, ask before escalating, and accept that maintainers may say no to a
  tool without that being a judgement of you.
- Respect the project's constraints even when you disagree with them. Argue for a change
  in an issue; don't smuggle it into a PR.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Sexual language or imagery, unwelcome attention, or personal attacks.
- Publishing others' private information.
- Deliberately adding code that collects, transmits or exposes user data.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere someone
is representing the project.

## Reporting

Report problems to the maintainers at [github.com/rapturedotsh](https://github.com/rapturedotsh)
by opening a private security advisory or contacting a maintainer directly. Reports are
handled discreetly. Maintainers may edit, remove or block, and will explain when they do.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org).
